# Codebook: the tables of the browser lab

The [browser lab](../lab.qmd) of the cohabitation case is a demonstration lab: apart from the reproduction of Task 3, which runs on the file of the analysts (`score_person_months.csv`, [codebook](score_person_months_codebook.md)), it fits no model and does not download the union file. The other numbers that its Tasks 2 and 3 print come from the eight small files below, each written by `R/cohabitation/06_displays.R` with the code that the downloadable workspace runs for the same task, so that the lab and the workspace give the same numbers. The results of Tasks 4 and 5 are the rows of `spec_curve.csv` ([codebook](spec_curve_codebook.md)); Model 2 of the paper and the change for each group behind it are rows of `display_options.csv` and `display_subgroups.csv` ([codebook](displays_codebook.md)); and the answers of the causal-graph task are in `dag_answers.csv` ([codebook](dag_answers_codebook.md)). The union file is `cohab_unions.csv` ([codebook](cohab_unions_codebook.md)).

## Task 2

- `lab_cycles.csv`: the unions of each of the five surveys (`cycle`, `unions`), 19,183 in all, as `datawizard::data_summary()` counts them on the union file.
- `lab_outcomes.csv`: the unions of the paper, those begun no more than five years before the interview, lasting at least a month, and in 2006–2010 begun in 2005 or later, by survey and by how they ended (`cycle`, `outcome`, `unions`): 929 of 1995 (340 marriages, 347 separations, 242 still intact) and 1,142 of 2006–2010 (194, 504 and 444).
- `lab_composition.csv`: for the unions of the paper in each survey, `unions` and the weighted shares, in per cent and rounded to one decimal, of Hispanic women (`hispanic`), Black women (`black`) and graduates (`college`), with the weighted mean age at which the couple moved in (`mean_age`).
- `lab_example.csv`: the person-months of one union of 1995 that became a marriage in its third month, as `person_months()` builds them (`cycle`, `caseid`, `month`, `y`): three rows, the last of which records the marriage.
- `lab_months.csv`: every person-month of the unions of the paper by how the month ended (`y`, `months`): 33,197 person-months, 534 of which record a marriage.
- `lab_married.csv`: the share of each cohort (`cohort`, begun 1990–1995 or 2005–2010) that had married by the end of each month since the couple moved in (`month`, 1 to 61 for the earlier cohort and 1 to 60 for the later one), from the Aalen-Johansen estimator of `survival::survfit()` with the survey weights, marriage and separation as competing outcomes and an intact union censored at the interview (`married`). By the end of the third year the share is 45.3% for the earlier cohort and 28.7% for the later one. These are the data of the figure of Task 2.

## Task 3

- `lab_score_check.csv`: the 929 unions of 1995 in the window of the paper, by how they ended (`outcome`) and by whether the case number of the woman appears among the unions of the person-month file of SCORE (`in_score_file`), with their number (`unions`). Of the 340 marriages, 2 are in the file; of the 347 separations, 346; of the 242 intact unions, 3.
- `lab_repaired.csv`: the person-months of the file of SCORE with its 1995 rows rebuilt from the 929 unions of the paper in the conventions of the file (an intact union contributes as many months as it lasted and a union that ended one more, with its outcome in the last), beside the rows of 2006–2010 of the file: `survey`, `marriages` (the months that record a marriage) and `months`. The lab runs `stats::prop.test()` on these counts, which gives $\chi^2$ = 87.68 on one degree of freedom.
