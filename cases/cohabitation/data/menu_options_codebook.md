# Codebook: `menu_options.csv`

`menu_options.csv` lists every option of the specification menu of the cohabitation case, one row per option, 48 rows over 15 decisions. For each option it records whether it is on the curve of the claim, the 144 specifications of `spec_curve.csv` ([codebook](spec_curve_codebook.md)), which page of the course teaches it, and the reasoning on whether it answers the claim under test, "cohabitors have increasingly become less likely to progress to marriage", in the wording that SCORE and Multi100 took from the paper of the original authors (OAs, p. 932). The file is written by hand, not by a pipeline stage; its rows are kept in `_planning/cohabitation/tools/write_menu_options.py`, which writes it. It follows the schema of the menu files of the other cases. The menu is a proposal for CM, set out with its evidence in `_planning/cohabitation/menu-audit.md`.

Three parts of the course read it. The decision map (`cases/cohabitation/map.qmd`) builds its stages, its selectable options, the estimand statement and the description of each option from it. The specification menu (`cases/cohabitation/menu.qmd`) builds its tables of the three levels from it. And the helpers of the browser lab and of the workspace read `axis`, `option`, `on_curve`, `taught_on` and `reason` to explain why an option off the curve is refused, so those five columns and the codes in them must not change without a matching change in the helpers.

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|:--|:--|
| `axis` | The decision, as a code: `comparison`, `cohorts`, `horizon`, `outcome`, `adjust`, `weights`, `sample` (Level 1); `histories`, `education`, `short` (Level 2); `unit`, `time`, `model`, `errors`, `scale` (Level 3). The six decisions that vary on the curve, `cohorts`, `horizon`, `adjust`, `weights`, `time` and `model`, carry the codes of the columns of `spec_curve.csv` and of the arguments of `fit_spec()` and `try_spec()`; `comparison`, `education`, `short` and `errors` are further arguments of `fit_spec()`, with the option of the curve as their default |
| `level` | `estimand`, `measurement` or `estimation`, the level of the menu at which the decision sits |
| `option` | The option code, as the curve, the deviation file and the helpers write it |
| `label` | A short label for the option, used on the decision map, in the tables of the menu and in messages |
| `on_curve` | `TRUE` for an option that the curve of 144 keeps (23 rows: the 14 options of the six varying decisions and the one option that each of the other nine decisions holds fixed), `FALSE` for one that the course teaches off the curve (25 rows) |
| `taught_on` | The page that teaches the option, as a path from the project root: `cases/cohabitation/index.qmd`, `cases/cohabitation/menu.qmd`, `cases/cohabitation/multiverse.qmd`, or one of the companion modules `companion/event-history.qmd`, `companion/binary-outcomes.qmd`, `companion/dags.qmd` and `companion/weighting.qmd`. A page in `cases/cohabitation/` links to it two levels up |
| `reason` | One sentence on why the option is or is not on the curve, used by the helpers in their messages and by the verdict of the decision map |
| `decision` | The name of the decision, as the decision map and the menu print it |
| `stage` | The stage of the decision map at which the decision is made: `comparison` (the surveys and the cohorts), `outcome` (what counts as progress to marriage, and over what time), `data` (the women and unions, their histories, the very short unions, the weights), `adjustment` (what the comparison holds constant, and the coding of education), `model` (the unit, the time since the start, the model) or `inference` (the errors and the effect scale) |
| `judgement` | The verdict on the option for this claim: `kept` (on the curve, a defensible answer; 23 rows); `other_question` (it answers a different question, about other cohorts, another outcome, a subgroup or another population; 9); `weaker_answer` (it answers this question, but less well than the options on the curve, or wrongly; 7); `check` (a diagnostic or another presentation of an answer that the curve already holds; 7); `unavailable` (it would answer the question, but the data cannot support it; 2: plans to marry, which the 1995 survey did not ask about, and men, whom the 1995 survey did not interview). The decision map labels the last "Not in the data" |
| `phrase` | The phrase that the option writes into the estimand statement of the decision map |
| `estimates` | What the option estimates, in one sentence |
| `explanation` | Two to four sentences on whether and why the option answers the claim, for the description that the decision map opens when the option is selected |

## The phrases of the estimand statement

The decision map can assemble the statement from the `phrase` column in a fixed order: "[Women and unions], [cohorts compared] [surveys compared], the change in [outcome] [time frame], [weights], [adjustment] ([union histories], [education], [very short unions])." The phrase of `sample` begins with "among", which the page capitalises. A second sentence carries the Level 3 decisions: "[Model] [unit of analysis], [time since the start], [standard errors] [effect scale]." The phrases of `model` begin with "Estimated by". The order is a proposal for the page that builds the map.

## Options with numbers elsewhere

Only the options on the curve are fitted across the curve. Eleven options off the curve are fitted once, at the reference settings, by `R/cohabitation/06_displays.R` into `display_options.csv` ([codebook](displays_codebook.md)): Models 1 and 2 of the paper (`educ_period`, `race_period`), the total length of the union as a control (`total_duration`), no time term (`time = "none"`), the linear probability model (`lpm`), the education of the SCORE file (`education = "score"`), the unions of less than a month (`short = "keep"`) and four other pairs of surveys (`comparison`). The errors clustered by woman are a column of the curve and, with the conventional errors, a row of `display_inference.csv`; the ratio and the partial correlation are columns of the curve (`ratio`, `r`). The share married within three years (`cumulative_3y`) is in `display_shares.csv` and `display_lifetable.csv`; the SCORE file (`score_file`), one row per union (`unions`), the test of proportions on rows (`rows_test`) and the trend over five surveys (`trend`) are the analyses of the five Multi100 analysts in `analysts5.csv` ([codebook](analysts5_codebook.md)), refitted on the SCORE file and on the same file with its 1995 cohort rebuilt. The share of unions that had become a marriage by the interview (`married_by_interview`) is a column of `display_shares.csv`, and three of the analysts fitted models of it. Four options have no number at the reference settings: separation as the outcome (its coefficients in Models 1 and 2 are in `display_reproduction.csv`), plans to marry, men, and every cohabitation.

## Editing

Keep the existing columns and codes, and keep `reason` to one sentence. Every prose column is participant-facing, so it follows the writing conventions of the course: British spelling, spaced en-dashes, double quotation marks for quotation and single ones for terms held at a distance, relative clauses written out in full, and no possessive on an institution or an artefact. A new option needs a row here, a decision in the helpers about how to treat it, and, if it joins the curve, an entry in `SPEC_AXES` of `R/cohabitation/_tools.R` and a refit of `spec_curve.csv` and `menu_deviations.csv`.
