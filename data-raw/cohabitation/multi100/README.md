# Multi100 record of the cohabitation claim

`multi100_kuo_extract.csv` holds the five rows of `multi100_processed_data.csv` for the paper `Kuo_Demography_2016_JWzJ`, one per analyst, with every Task 1 and Task 2 field and the cleaned effect sizes. The columns that carry analysts' names, sign-up details and timestamps are left out. The full file is public at github.com/marton-balazs-kovacs/multi100 (mirrored at osf.io/q5h2c); the extract was taken on 2026-10-26 from the copy in `_planning/housing/sources/`. The record gives the original result as z = −4.8786 on 54,291 person-months (r = −0.0209), from the coefficient −0.683 (SE 0.14) of Table 2 of the paper.

The project is reported in Aczel et al. (2026), "Investigating the analytical robustness of the social and behavioural sciences", *Nature*, doi:10.1038/s41586-025-09844-9. Its conversion rules, which `R/cohabitation/_tools.R` follows in `r_multi100()`: a t statistic gives r = t / sqrt(t^2 + df); an F statistic with one numerator degree of freedom gives r = sqrt(F / (F + df2)); a z statistic gives r = tanh(|z| × sqrt(1 / (N − 3))) × sign(z), the sample size standing in for the missing degrees of freedom; a chi-square is read as the square of a z and carries no sign.

The analysts' scripts and reports are on OSF, in the Multi100 component of the paper (osf.io/b69nv): 73FDT osf.io/k7zq2 (R and R Markdown, with the five coefficients in `estimates.csv`), N8ZYW osf.io/dymz4 (jamovi files and notes), 456AP osf.io/2m38h (R), 5X3KG osf.io/q6skv (R Markdown), JDA15 osf.io/uxgzp (R). All five analysed the person-month file that SCORE built for its replication (`Estimation Data - Kuo (JWzJ).csv`, osf.io/b6p3j), which `R/cohabitation/04_reference_marks.R` fetches into the gitignored cache.
