# Committed inputs of the immigration-and-trust case

Small tables that the pipeline in `R/trust/` needs besides the ESS files, which stay in the data hub and are read only by `R/trust/01_extract.R`. Nothing here holds survey responses. Fetched tables were retrieved on 2026-10-25; a snapshot is fetched again only when its file is deleted.

| File | What it holds | Source and licence |
|---|---|---|
| `mclaren_appendix.csv` | The web appendix of the paper, transcribed by hand: for each country and round of the ESS, the year in which fieldwork started, the share of the vote for far-right (anti-immigration) parties at the last general election, and those parties. Five country-rounds are "N/A" in the appendix (Austria 4, Greece 3, Ireland 4, Italy 3 and 4) and are left empty. | McLaren, Lauren M. 2011. Web appendix to "The Cultural Divide in Europe", eprints.nottingham.ac.uk/1566 (copy in the SCORE dossier, osf.io/j5fhy). Facts transcribed from a published table. |
| `party_codes.csv` | Every party code of the ESS questions on the party voted for at the last national election, rounds 1–4, in the 16 countries (725 codes): the party, whether the code is technical (other, blank, refused), its Party Facts and ParlGov keys, and whether the appendix of the paper counts the party as far right. Written by `R/trust/00_party_codes.R`. | Party Facts crosswalk of ESS party codes (Bederke and Döring, `hdigital/partyfactsdata`, commit f336bca, MIT licence) and Party Facts external keys (partyfacts.herokuapp.com); the Italian round 2, which Party Facts does not cover, follows the codes of round 1. Thirteen links between ESS codes and ParlGov cabinet parties are made by hand in the script (electoral alliances and renamed parties), each with its reason. |
| `cabinets.csv` | The cabinets of the 16 countries in office from 1 January 2001 to 2010, with their start dates and member parties (ParlGov keys). Written by `R/trust/00_party_codes.R`. | ParlGov development version (Döring, Quaas, Hesse and Manow 2023, parlgov.org), `view_cabinet.csv`. The data page states no licence; the stable releases on Harvard Dataverse are published under CC BY-SA 4.0. |
| `worldbank_wgi.csv` | The six Worldwide Governance Indicators (estimates, about −2.5 to 2.5) for the 16 countries, 2000–2009; 2001 is missing because the indicators were biennial until 2002. Written by `R/trust/02_macro.R`. | World Bank, Worldwide Governance Indicators (API source 3, indicators `GOV_WGI_*.EST`), CC BY 4.0. |
| `worldbank_wdi.csv` | GDP per head at purchasing power parity in current international dollars (`NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.CD`) and unemployment as a share of the labour force, modelled ILO estimate (`SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS`), for the 16 countries and the OECD members as a group (`OED`), 2000–2009. Written by `R/trust/02_macro.R`. | World Bank, World Development Indicators, CC BY 4.0. |
| `eurostat_social_protection.csv` | Total expenditure on social protection, euro per inhabitant at current prices, 2000–2009 (`spr_exp_type`, `spdeps = TOTAL`, `unit = EUR_HAB`). Written by `R/trust/02_macro.R`. | Eurostat, ESSPROS; reuse authorised with acknowledgement of the source (Eurostat copyright notice). |
| `multi100/multi100_mclaren_extract.csv` | The five rows of the Multi100 record for the paper (`McLaren_WorldPolitics_2012_wRvv`), one per analyst, without the columns that carry names, sign-up details and timestamps. See `multi100/README.md`. | Multi100 processed data (github.com/marton-balazs-kovacs/multi100). |

The paper names OECD series for GDP per head and unemployment; the course takes them from the World Bank, which covers all 16 countries for every year, as the SCORE reproduction from source data did for unemployment. GDP per head enters as an index on which the OECD members average 100, as the paper describes it ("using the average OECD gdp/capita as the base").

`cache/` (gitignored) holds the Party Facts and ParlGov tables that built the two party tables, the analysis file that Multi100 gave the analysts (`McLaren_SRD_final_data.csv`, osf.io/e48gd, person-level ESS data under an unstated licence), and a copy of the course extract with respondent numbers. None of it may be committed.
