# Multi100 record of the immigration-and-trust claim

`multi100_mclaren_extract.csv` holds the five rows of `multi100_processed_data.csv` for the paper `McLaren_WorldPolitics_2012_wRvv`, one per analyst, with every Task 1 and Task 2 field and the cleaned effect sizes. The columns that carry analysts' names, sign-up details and timestamps are left out. The full file is public at github.com/marton-balazs-kovacs/multi100 (mirrored at osf.io/q5h2c); the extract was taken on 2026-10-25 from the copy in `_planning/housing/sources/`. The record holds no statistic for the original result (`original_effect_size_available` is "No").

The project is reported in Aczel et al. (2026), "Investigating the analytical robustness of the social and behavioural sciences", *Nature*, doi:10.1038/s41586-025-09844-9. Its conversion rules, which `R/trust/_tools.R` follows in `r_multi100()`: a t statistic gives r = t / sqrt(t^2 + df); an F statistic with one numerator degree of freedom gives r = sqrt(F / (F + df2)); a z statistic gives r = tanh(|z| × sqrt(1 / (N − 3))) × sign(z), the sample size standing in for the missing degrees of freedom.

The analysts' own scripts and reports are on OSF, in the Multi100 component of the paper (osf.io/9jvbt): N8P2J osf.io/npavt (R Markdown), FQ1G7 osf.io/q8r57 (reports only; the analysis ran in jamovi), LT39B osf.io/y465m (Stata), 456AD osf.io/e7pbq (Stata), 6U3LL osf.io/s7t9d (R, with logs). All five analysed the file that the SCORE source-data reproduction 65wm6 had built from ESS rounds 1–4 (`McLaren_SRD_final_data.csv`, osf.io/e48gd), which `R/trust/04_reference_marks.R` fetches into the gitignored cache.
